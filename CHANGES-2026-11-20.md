The database change (payments.user_id, payer_name, payer_email) has already been applied in Supabase.

1. Sign-up without email confirmation (/signup): account is created and signed in immediately. Required tick box
   "I confirm my details are correct". One email = one account; an existing email is sent to Sign in with a reset-password link.
2. Separate pages: /login (sign in only), /signup, /forgot-password, /reset-password, /pay/<service>, /pay/status/<reference>.
   Each page returns people to where they came from (?next= / ?back=).
3. Forgot password: sends a reset link only to the registered email. Optional but recommended: in Supabase >
   Authentication > Email Templates > Reset Password, paste supabase/reset-password-email.html (link works on any device).
   In Supabase > Authentication > URL Configuration > Redirect URLs, add https://tigermaxjobs.com/** and https://www.tigermaxjobs.com/**
4. Payments: offshore registration, Winning CV (US$15) and Know yourself + CV revamp (US$65) each have their own page.
   After Paynow, /pay/status checks the payment with Paynow itself, so payments show as paid even if Paynow's callback was missed.
   Employer listing payments also return to /pay/status.
5. Adverts on the candidate dashboard and the jobs page: psychometric tests (links to /assessments) and CV writing with
   "Tap here to pay US$65 / US$15" buttons.
6. Office page: new "CV writing orders paid" table (name, email, phone, reference) – contact each client within one working day.
